# Assetgrove logo package

The mark is a painter's palette cut from a log slice: natural tree rings inside a bark rim, a thumb hole, four dabs of paint, a twig paintbrush with a brass ferrule resting across it and hanging off the edge, and one leaf at the rim.

## Master files

- `master/assetgrove-mark-original.png`: the approved artwork with its transparency, untouched.
- `master/assetgrove-mark-transparent-1024.png`: the mark alone on transparency, centred in a 1024 px square.
- `master/assetgrove-icon-cream-1024.png`: the primary icon on warm cream.
- `master/assetgrove-icon-slate-1024.png`: full-bleed square icon on slate, for platforms that apply their own corner mask.

## Web files

- `web/favicon.ico`: bundled 16, 32 and 48 px favicon.
- `web/favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png`: the mark alone, at each size.
- `web/apple-touch-icon.png`: 180 px, on cream.
- `web/icon-192.png`, `web/icon-512.png`: web-app manifest sizes, on cream.

## Usage notes

- Use the cream icon as the primary app, listing and avatar treatment; slate where a dark surface needs more contrast.
- Use the transparent mark over quiet cream, off-white or slate surfaces.
- Keep the painted texture and the irregular rings: do not trace the mark into flat vector art.
- `preview/assetgrove-logo-preview.png` shows the mark, both icons and the small sizes together.
